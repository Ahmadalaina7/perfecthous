How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

A renovation is disruptive enough. The way we work should not be: you know beforehand what happens, who is coming and what it costs.

“What pleased me most is that difficult points were raised at the survey, instead of turning up later as extra work.”

Marc de Ruiter — Oost-Souburg

  1. 01

    Introduction and survey

    We visit, walk the space with you and listen to what you want. No sales pitch, but honest questions about budget and schedule.

  2. 02

    A clear quote

    Within a few working days you receive a quote with line items, materials and an indicative schedule. What is not in it is deliberately left out.

  3. 03

    One point of contact during the work

    The same person who did the survey also runs the work. You never have to switch between parties; we handle the order and the coordination.

  4. 04

    Handover and aftercare

    We review the work together, you get the warranty on paper and a few weeks later we call to ask whether everything is to your liking. Small aftercare is not an extra assignment for us.
